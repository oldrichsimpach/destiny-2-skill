# Activities, enemies and secrets

For raids, dungeons, strikes, campaigns, exotic missions, seasonal/episodic missions, patrols, events, Gambit and PvP, identify exact variant, era and current availability. Distinguish a permanent encounter from a rotating variant; verify current rotations and reset boundaries live rather than assuming a historical schedule persists.

For an encounter capture objective, stages, wipe/failure conditions, roles, timers, safe damage windows, shields/immunities, target movement and crit behavior, range, hazards, adds, champions, factions and special enemy variants. Enemy presence belongs to a named encounter/difficulty/rotation; a faction appearing somewhere in a destination does not prove a specific unit appears in every activity there. Cite observed or documented locations and mark unverified variants.

Acquisition guides need exact reward/roll variant, prerequisites, quest steps, access requirements, source encounter/vendor, rotation, lockout/farming rules and whether rewards are guaranteed or probabilistic. Never invent drop rates. Distinguish an old acquisition route from today's route and a collectible entry from an obtainable item.

Secrets and puzzles: give prerequisites, location, trigger, ordered steps, reproducible confirmation and reward. Separate confirmed solution, active hypothesis, historical exploit, patched method and rumor. For removed content provide historical context and availability; do not direct the user to inaccessible steps as if playable. Include spoilers at the level the user requested; a general build question does not need puzzle spoilers.

Link encounter requirements to the quantitative build: immunity or no crit spot can invalidate a paper DPS winner; mobile targets reduce projectile accuracy; short phases favor burst; dense adds can enable kill-based loops that fail in isolated boss phases; solo survival may dominate team-only buff ceilings. Confirm these mechanics for the specific activity rather than applying generic faction stereotypes.
