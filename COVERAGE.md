# Coverage — 0.4-beta.1

| Requested area | Included | Remaining boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence/conflicts | 82 scoped records, source lineage, strict patch/unit/component/condition resolution and conflict variants | Not every effect or historical patch; unknown applicability blocks numerical use |
| Boss Damage | Indexed/readable permitted originals, exact row notes/formulas, candidate normalized baseline export, 47 archetype cross-checks | Not every rotation independently measured/revalidated |
| Legal combinations | Exact manifest hashes/sockets, class/slot/unique-label groups; owned evidence paths; optional audited subclass/artifact/armor policies | Server conditions, actual ownership and full rule corpus remain unknown unless supplied |
| Rotations/sensitivity | Finite event/action model, ammo/refills, effects/procs, movement/range and seeded Monte Carlo | Explicit timing/count semantics; no inferred geometry or complete engine mechanics |
| Survival/abilities | Typed incoming schedules, DR groups/pools/healing; sequential charges, scalars and causal refunds | Needs measured input coefficients, attack schedules and applicable special rules |
| Source changes | Snapshot/patch review, dependent-result discovery and persisted invalidations | Conservative watches/mentions; human semantic review; literals not automatically tracked |
| Optimization | Owned-instance filtering and Pareto comparison within supplied candidates | No global enumeration, universal utility score or automatic inventory account access |

Existing v0.3 Endgame audit, 2,495-location index, v0.2 effect/fact/stat subsets and manifest helper are preserved. Old data retain their old evidence dates and patch restrictions. Source XLSX readers need openpyxl. Current source details and private workbook hashes are in skill data metadata; no full source mirrors are publicly redistributed.

All six areas have concrete implementations and exercised examples in this beta. This coverage table does not certify every real Destiny build, source value, current sandbox or legal restriction.
